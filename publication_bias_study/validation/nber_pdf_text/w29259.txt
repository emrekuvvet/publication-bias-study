ABSTRACT
This is an invited chapter for the forthcoming Volume 4 of the Handbook of Industrial
Organization. We focus on markets with frictions, such as transaction costs, asymmetric
information, search and matching frictions. We discuss how such frictions affect allocations,
favor the emergence of intermediaries or dealers, and potentially create market power. Our focus
is mostly on markets with many participants rather than on transactions that are bilateral or
involve a small number of players.
